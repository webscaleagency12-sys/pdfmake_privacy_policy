1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which WebScale Agency ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("you" or "User") of the PDF Maker: Scan & Convert PDF Android mobile application (the "App").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many cloud-dependent utilities, PDF Maker does not require user registration or personal accounts. We do NOT collect, store, or sell any of the following:

  • No User Account Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, or passwords.
  • No Personal Files or Documents: We never transmit or upload your photos, camera captures, text documents, or generated PDFs to any server.
  • No Biometrics: We do not scan, capture, or store facial recognition or biometric data.
  • No Precise Location Data: We never request or access GPS or fine-grained location services.
  • No Contacts, Calendar, or Microphones: We never access your address book, call logs, calendar events, or audio recorders.

3. Device Permissions and How They Are Used

To deliver core scanning and conversion functionality, the App requests minimal Android system permissions strictly in accordance with Google Play's Least-Privilege guidelines:

Permission Type Purpose & Data Handling
android.permission.CAMERA Runtime (Dangerous) Document Scanning: Required only when you use the "Scan Document" or "Smart Scan" features to capture physical documents, receipts, or notes using your device's camera. The captured image frames are processed entirely in memory on your device to detect edges, crop, and compile into a PDF. Images are never transmitted over the internet.
Photo Picker / Storage Zero-Permission / Scoped Images to PDF: Allows you to pick images from your device gallery to convert them into PDF files. Uses Android's native system Photo Picker (`PickVisualMedia`) without requiring broad storage access permissions.
android.permission.INTERNET Install-Time (Normal) Ad Delivery: Used by the third-party Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to fetch and display banner advertisements within the app.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-Time (Normal) Connection Status: Allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to determine whether an active internet connection is available before attempting to request banner ads.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App includes banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To display relevant advertisements, prevent ad fraud, and generate ad delivery metrics, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personal and pseudonymous device data, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID / AAID).
  • IP Address & Network Information: Used to estimate coarse (country/city level) location and verify connection integrity.
  • App Interaction & Performance Data: Ad views, clicks, interaction timestamps, diagnostic crash logs, and SDK performance statistics.

For detailed information on how Google collects, protects, and processes advertising data, please review Google's official privacy documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

How You Can Control or Reset Advertising Data

You have full control over personalized advertising on your Android device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: Go to your Android device Settings → Google → All services → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) and tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads: Visit Google Ad Settings to manage your ad preferences.

6. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Local Storage: All PDF documents created by the App are saved inside your device's private app storage (sandboxed inside context.filesDir/generated_pdfs) or explicitly exported to your device's public storage upon your request.

User Control & Deletion: You retain complete ownership and control over your files:

  • You can view and delete individual PDFs at any time from the "My PDFs" screen in the App.
  • You can clear all app storage and generated files via Settings → Storage Management → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the App from your device permanently removes all locally stored PDFs and cache files created by the App.

  • No unauthorized cloud endpoints or third-party tracking libraries are integrated into the application.
